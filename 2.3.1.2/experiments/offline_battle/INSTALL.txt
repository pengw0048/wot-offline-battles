World of Tanks 2.3.1.2 offline battle 0.6.0
===========================================

1. Remove earlier offline probe or battle packages from:
     <World_of_Tanks>\mods\2.3.1.2\

2. Copy this file there:
     org.peng.offline_2312_battle_0.6.0.wotmod

3. Run from the client root:
     win64\WorldOfTanks.exe --script-arg offlineBattle --script-arg offline --script-arg spaces/01_karelia

Expected result: Karelia loads, an MS-1 spawns on the team-one CTF spawn
facing the enemy base, and the stock ArcadeCamera attaches to it. W/A/S/D
drives the tank, the mouse rotates the view and the turret. Close the game
window (or Alt+F4) to exit; python.log should show `shutdown_complete` and
no Traceback.

Key python.log markers, in order:
  route_enter
  gameplay_machine_started
  session_started        (real BattleSessionProvider + AvatarInputHandler)
  vehicle_created        (real Vehicle entity on the spawn terrain)
  player_vehicle_selected
  client_ready_requested
  arena_period_pushed
  battle_ready           (filter/appearance/input/camera summary)

If the run stalls, `bootstrap_failed` or `bootstrap_timeout` names the stage
and the avatar init-progress bits.

Not implemented yet in this slice: shooting authority, bots, damage. The
`vehicle_shoot` request is accepted and logged but fires no projectile.

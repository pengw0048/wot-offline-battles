# wot-0.9.22-offline-battles v0.9.1

This release includes all updates since v0.9.0 for the Chinese HD
World of Tanks 0.9.22.0.1 #1513 client.

## Scenery collisions and vehicle movement

- Improve collision handling for destructible buildings, fences, railings,
  walls, parked vehicles and other map props across the supported maps.
- Remove lingering collision from destroyed original components while retaining
  the collision of their visible replacements, intact neighbouring components
  and solid walls behind them. Destruction is resolved per component, including
  overlapping and shared fence surfaces.
- Fix residual invisible barriers around Malinovka fences, including tilted
  fence sections and small inaccuracies in the original surface normals.
- Resolve accepted scenery destruction in the same movement update, improving
  continuity when driving through breakable objects.
- Allow powered hull turns to break eligible objects, including the reported
  Paris props, without requiring forward movement.
- Use the actual vehicle footprint and contact position for scenery checks,
  preventing premature stops before reaching an object.
- Improve turning away from an existing contact and glancing movement along
  hard walls. The complete turn remains checked for new obstructions.
- Apply the shared collision improvements to both player vehicles and Bots.

## Terrain, pavement and detached turrets

- Fix slope and crest departure contacts that could pin vehicles while moving
  downhill, including tanks with hydraulic suspension.
- Improve crossings between road surfaces and raised pavement in Paris.
- Fix support selection when a tilted vehicle enters a concrete platform or
  crosses its edge, reducing cases where a track drops into the surface or the
  vehicle becomes stuck. Grounded player vehicles and Bots use the same support
  limits.
- Correct collision between vehicle hulls and the gun barrels of detached
  turrets, including thin barrels resting at track height.

## Bot navigation

- Align the Prague factory route with the centres of the workshop doorways,
  with updated routes for both teams.
- Improve route planning and shortcuts near observed solid obstructions using
  shared native collision checks and vehicle-width clearance. Navigation
  updates these checks when scenery is destroyed.

## Personal missions and battle results

- Record damage events separately from damage totals, allowing missions such
  as MT-2 to count repeated damaging hits correctly across all four operations.
- Add recorded event timing, pre-hit immobilization, kill distance and track
  destruction evidence for the relevant MT-3, MT-4 and HT-2 conditions,
  including their additional objectives.
- Expand SPG mission support with stun counts, distinct stunned targets, stun
  duration, damage events, critical hits, remaining unspotted and kills assisted
  by stun or track immobilization.
- Count shots that stun two or three targets for multi-stun objectives.
- Preserve fractional stun duration and count valid zero-damage stuns. Healing,
  overlapping stuns and timer expiry retain the events already earned.
- Carry the new statistics through the server, saved battle results and mission
  settlement. Retransmitted events and repeated settlement do not award them
  twice; friendly targets are excluded from enemy-only objectives.
- Show stun statistics in battle results and include missing mission-condition
  evidence in diagnostic logs.

## Vehicle prices and LAN entry

- Correct tech-tree and catalogue gold prices, including 32,000 gold for 121B
  and 9,000 gold for Panzer 58 Mutz, while respecting catalogue availability.
- Keep Special Offers bond quotes separate from ordinary vehicle prices.
  The displayed purchase route now determines the currency and purchase terms:
  bond offers include a garage slot and trained crew, while ordinary catalogue
  purchases use their normal terms.
- Route the garage's Create Platoon entries into the LAN room selected in the
  launcher. Players can enter through Battle! or Create Platoon and select the
  same team in the waiting room.
- Update the installation and launcher instructions for hosting, joining and
  playing together in a LAN room.

## Diagnostics and package

- Add more detailed collision evidence, including native material candidates,
  contact normals and support layers, to help identify map obstructions.
- Add detached-turret geometry and Bot hydraulic-suspension state to relevant
  diagnostic logs.
- Update the launcher, bundled client mod and LAN server to version 0.9.1.
  The Windows x64 download includes these complete English release notes as
  `UPDATE_NOTES.md`.

## Download

Download `wot-0.9.22-offline-battles-0.9.1-Windows-x64.zip`, extract it into an
empty folder, and run `wot-0.9.22-offline-battles.exe`. Select the supported game
folder and install the bundled client mod before starting a game.

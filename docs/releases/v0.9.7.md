# Version 0.9.7

This release brings together the changes tested since v0.9.6: corrected crew hit feedback, better steering and collision handling, lighter Bot ground support, bridge and visibility fixes, restored Bot audio, crew service records, offline replay recording and playback, larger LAN state transfers, and automatic session cleanup when the game exits.

Supported game client: **Chinese HD World of Tanks 0.9.22.0.1 #1513, 32-bit**. The launcher and bundled server require 64-bit Windows.

## Crew voices and shell impact feedback

- Correct HE voice selection. A direct damaging HE hit no longer incorrectly uses the splash-damage voice group.
- Use the following outcomes consistently. Voice examples describe the meaning of the Chinese client's lines, not a replacement English voice pack.

| HE result | Crew feedback | Impact effects and sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct hit, no penetration, but HP damage | Critical/direct-hit group, such as “Critical hit” or “We hit them hard” | HE explosion |
| No direct hit; splash causes HP damage | Splash group, such as “A little more aim would have helped” or “Target covered” | HE explosion |
| Direct penetrating hit with HP damage | Penetration group | HE explosion |
| Direct hit with no HP damage | Non-penetration group, such as “We failed to penetrate their armour” | HE non-penetration effect and sound |

- AP, APCR and HEAT hits that actually damage a module or injure a crew member now use critical-hit feedback even when they cause no HP damage. Selection no longer depends only on penetration and HP loss.
- Preserve higher-priority destruction/fire feedback and the actual damage result. These corrections do not change shell penetration, damage or vehicle statistics. The HE impact-effects work already present in v0.9.6 is retained; the crew-voice correction is part of v0.9.7.

## Steering, slopes and contact damage

- Correct the engine-power allocation between driving and turning. Full forward drive could consume the available budget and leave insufficient turning force when a vehicle was against a slope, another tank or a wreck.
- Improve escape from those contacts, including turning while moving away. Straight-line driving is preserved; acceleration while driving and turning together reflects the shared power budget.
- Correct cases where traversable slope contact was treated as a wall.
- Improve the force application point when hulls of different lengths collide, and validate rotation together with translation.
- Preserve the first-contact motion evidence needed for ram damage across updates. Fix missed damage caused by mismatched motion histories or discarded contact evidence, including cases after touching a wreck.
- Keep one damage event per actual impact and the existing rule that friendly ramming causes no HP damage.

## Bot ground support and bridge departures

- Reduce hidden-worker ground-support overhead for Bots and Bot wrecks by replacing their detailed multi-spring support path with a simpler support model. The player's full suspension path is unchanged.
- Preserve gravity, airborne motion, landing/fall handling, horizontal collisions, pushing and contact damage. Complex terrain, one-sided track support and wreck pitch/roll are more approximate under this model.
- Fix an invalid bridge/navigation slope value being interpreted as an almost vertical ramp and injecting upward momentum. Vertical motion now follows measured support-height changes, preventing the reported bridge launches from this invalid probe result.

These changes address the reproduced cases; they do not certify every bridge, cliff or native physics interaction on every map.

## Visibility and audio

- Remove the moving, water-like shimmer left behind after an enemy Bot becomes unspotted. Attached effects, callbacks and effect timelines now stop with the hidden presentation.
- Restore Bot engine and gun sounds after visibility changes, including explicit activation of newly created sound components.
- Keep unspotted enemies inaudible and the hidden simulation client muted.
- Restore replay engine RPM/gear audio and repeated gunshot/muzzle effects with the correct sound/effect lifecycle.
- Give reload audio one controller and coalesce excessively dense mechanical prompts. Actual ammunition, magazine capacity, firing rate and reload timing are unchanged.

### Spotting rule clarification

**The 50-metre proximity-spotting rule already existed in v0.9.6; it was missing from that release's notes. It is documented here, not introduced by v0.9.7.** Within 50 metres, proximity spotting does not depend on camouflage, bushes or line of sight through walls. Beyond that distance, normal view-range, camouflage and line-of-sight checks apply, with the ordinary 445-metre spotting cap.

## Crew service records

- Save battle counts and the personal awards supported by the client for the crew members who actually participated, together with their experience settlement.
- Preserve those records through barracks transfers and ordinary retraining, and avoid duplicate credit when settlement is retried.
- Do not award participation to replacement crew who did not fight. Training rooms do not grant these service awards, and old battles without a reliable participant roster are not retroactively credited.

## Offline replay recording and playback

- Add **Off / Last / All** recording options. Recordings use `.wotlanreplay` files under the game's `replays/offline` folder.
- Add the launcher's **Replay** tab. Select a recording and start playback; it loads automatically after garage initialization.
- Playback does not start a battle server or hidden Bot worker and does not grant rewards or advance battle progress.
- Complete the initial replay snapshot before the ready barrier, fixing the reported “waiting for players” deadlock.
- Apply recorded turret/gun poses through the supported native interface, retain manual camera control, and interpolate vehicle movement. Live mouse aiming no longer overwrites the recorded turret pose.
- Use recorded ammunition and reload state rather than resetting or running competing reload timers.
- Normalize native vector representations during vehicle-profile verification to prevent false mismatches. Genuine profile mismatches remain rejected.
- Move recording compression/writes and most playback decoding into separate lightweight processes using the bundled runtime; players do not need to install Python.
- Replace the saved **Last** recording only after the new recording completes successfully. Failed/incomplete recordings do not overwrite the previous complete recording; partial `.part` files may remain.

Current playback supports **forward 1x and a manual camera**. Pause, speed controls, seeking/rewinding and export to the retail `.wotreplay` format are not available. Use new recordings from this version and retain the vehicle profile used to record them.

## Large LAN state transfers

- Split large loading states, snapshots and mandatory updates into bounded fragments, shared by the visible client and authoritative worker paths.
- Apply a transferred state only after all its fragments are present and validated. This fixes message-size failures encountered with more complex team/vehicle data.
- Update all players and the host together, then restart the room/server.

## Exiting the game

- Link normal visible-game shutdown to cleanup of that session's hidden worker and temporary server. The reported leftover World of Tanks process no longer requires pressing the launcher's manual close button after exiting the game.
- Allow normal resource cleanup and pending recording writes to finish before retiring a lingering process group. Only the game process tracked for that launch can signal completion; replacement-client handoff and unrelated game sessions remain protected.
- Replay completion or leaving replay playback closes that game session while keeping the launcher open.
- An explicitly hosted, persistent LAN room retains its own lifetime: stop it with **Stop room** or by exiting the launcher. One player's departure does not terminate other players' room.

## Clean release package and installation

- Set the launcher, client mod and server release version to **0.9.7**.
- Remove loose test tools, historical test notes, diagnostic entry points and temporary files from the player distribution. Keep the executable, `_internal`, `licenses`, player README and license notices together.
- Source code and development/validation material are provided separately from the player ZIP.

Close the old game and launcher. Extract **wot-0.9.22-offline-battles-0.9.7-Windows-x64.zip** into a new empty folder, run the launcher and select the supported game client. Keep your existing save, recordings, settings and vehicle profiles; no reset is required. Do not mix old and new `_internal` folders or manually install another copy of the mod.

The Windows release is built from the tagged main-branch source. Packaging checks cover version alignment, x64 launcher/x86 starter, the client payload, recorder resources, installation and bundled-server startup. Focused regression and native Windows process-lifecycle tests accompany the fixes. User playtesting confirmed the final sound, visibility and shutdown changes; automated checks do not replace gameplay acceptance on every vehicle and map.

Outstanding internal-module geometry work from the v0.9.6 inventory, some map obstructions and existing unrelated regression-suite failures are not claimed resolved by this release.

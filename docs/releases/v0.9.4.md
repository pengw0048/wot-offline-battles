# wot-0.9.22-offline-battles v0.9.4

This is the cumulative changelog from **v0.9.3 to v0.9.4**, covering the changes retained in the published release, not just the final Bot rollback. It includes personal-mission repairs, directive purchases, spotting and radio integration, collision and startup fixes, and network command delivery.

The release uses the maintainer-selected **`a3e4c593` original-policy Bot build**, based on upstream v0.7.7. The separate **no-yield experiment is not included**. Supported game client: Chinese HD **World of Tanks 0.9.22.0.1 #1513**.

## Personal missions

- **LT-12: assistance while unspotted.** Support `damageAssistedRadioWhileInvisible`. Record each awarded radio-assistance share together with whether the observer was visible to the enemy at that instant. Do not substitute total spotting assistance, end-of-battle visibility, or the delayed Sixth Sense display. Evaluate the applicable seasonal camouflage and camouflage-net requirements from the battle-start loadout.
- **HT-12: maximum-health comparisons.** Support `compareWithMaxHealth` using the vehicle's battle-start maximum HP. Evaluate the installed mission's required damage/blocked-damage expression against that value, not remaining HP or an assumed stock-vehicle value.
- **TD-7: kills at full health.** Support `whileFullHealth` using the attacker's HP at the qualifying kill, rather than HP at the end of the battle. Settle reciprocal ramming damage and all effects of the same shell, including self-splash, before finalizing the full-health fact. Fire kills use the attacker's health when the victim dies.
- **TD-8: damage relative to maximum HP.** Apply the same maximum-health comparison support to the damage-dealt multiples required by the installed mission definition.
- **TD-9: consecutive penetrating shots.** Implement `inBattleMaxPiercingSeries`. Track shots in launch order so delayed impacts cannot create a false streak. Misses, non-penetrations, ricochets and expired projectiles interrupt the sequence; splash, friendly hits and wreck hits do not count as qualifying enemy penetrations.
- Cover the relevant primary and secondary conditions across all four operations. Continue reading the installed client's mission definitions; mission thresholds, rewards and operation vehicles are not changed.

## Mission evidence and battle-result persistence

- Extend the bounded mission-event history with unspotted radio-assistance events and full-health-at-kill evidence. Preserve battle-start maximum HP, seasonal outfits and the longest penetration sequence through server receipts, client validation, save/restart and post-battle evaluation.
- Keep older receipt formats readable without inventing missing evidence. Missing or incomplete historical data cannot retroactively satisfy a new condition. Validate penetration streaks against recorded shots and penetrations, and retain existing duplicate-receipt protections.
- Preserve earlier mission fixes already present in v0.9.3, including MT-11 ramming support. They are not advertised as newly implemented in this release; their receipt-compatibility regressions were updated for the extended event schema.

## Battle directives and garage purchases

- Fix bond-purchased battle directives being rejected as ordinary consumables during purchase and fitting. Select the directive slot from the installed item's equipment type instead of trusting an incompatible UI slot index.
- Buying or fitting one item now changes only that slot's desired resupply layout. Other consumed items keep their automatic-resupply targets and currency selections instead of being erased by the current mounted loadout.
- Preserve inventory, currency and fitting validation, including insufficient-funds and invalid-item failures. Ordinary consumables retain their normal slot rules.

## Bot behavior and original maps

- Restore upstream v0.7.7 navigation, driving, tactical integration, traffic coordination and Bot-owned runtime behavior instead of extending the later avoidance and replanning layers.
- Retain the original author's short crossing yields and head-on coordination. This is the selected **original-policy** version, not a blanket removal of vehicle avoidance or physical collision.
- Remove the later broad native-static corridor review, live static-grid repair, additional traffic-braking layer and predicted rotation-veto additions from the shipped Bot behavior.
- Use all **41 original navigation maps**, formations and routes, including the original Airfield graph. Temporary Airfield rebakes and subsequently reverted Bot recovery/support experiments are not release features.
- Retain the current shared physics, garage, personal missions, economy and networking systems. This is a Bot-policy rollback, not a downgrade of the entire game to upstream v0.7.7.

## Destructible classification in navigation

- Separate route planning from physical destruction eligibility. Filter the pinned client's original destructible materials from the **first** native planning query, including forward-corridor and navigation-ground queries.
- Do not require a stationary Bot to register each distant prop, prove sufficient current impact speed, or perform a chain of per-object collision recasts before ignoring original destructible surfaces for planning.
- Keep real terrain, hard scenery and remaining/replacement geometry in the collision query. Actual hull movement and destruction retain their physical contact checks; this does not authorize driving through intact hard walls.

## Spotting, radio and teammate presentation

- Preserve direct radio links and recipient-specific spotted contacts when using the restored Bot runtime. Fix the rollback incompatibility that cleared teammate radio knowledge and made allies appear dependent on the local player's direct sight.
- Carry the validated radio-recipient metadata through the server relay instead of dropping it during contact projection. Preserve observer-specific visibility leases, radio-loss handling and round-state cleanup.
- Keep map knowledge separate from model drawing distance. Radio contact can provide teammate or spotted-enemy positions without the local player spotting them directly; it does not reveal enemies that nobody has legitimately detected.
- Fix already-destroyed props continuing to block spotting rays when their original compiled collision skin uses a different item slot from the repaired live object. Reuse bounded ownership checks for the destroyed component while preserving the sight-specific collision mask. Intact components, unidentified surfaces and backing walls remain blockers.
- Existing shared-assistance allocation is retained. This release does not claim a newly reconstructed proprietary 0.9.22 assistance-rounding or attribution formula.

## Hydraulic startup, support and physical collision

- Initialize and reset the hydraulic legacy-support sample at construction, battle start and stop. This prevents the first hydraulic drive step from reading an uninitialized value or reusing support from the preceding map.
- Use the near-body support query for Bot physical height and attitude, rather than the broad placement column that could select an overhead bridge and reject otherwise clear movement.
- Preserve actual pitch and roll during native wall checks for an offset track pivot. Flattening that pose could place the raised side's collision rays into a slope below the hull.
- Add destination-perimeter checks to cross-heading sliding movement so a building corner cannot enter the middle of a long hull side between the existing corner rays.
- Allow separation from an already intersecting near-vertical wall only when the movement reduces penetration and satisfies the existing hull/face constraints. A second wall, inward motion or crossing through the building remains blocked. This is not a guarantee that every previously wedged pose can escape.

## LAN command delivery and battle startup

- Preserve sparse Bot-order updates when consecutive movement snapshots are coalesced. A newer movement-only snapshot must not discard an undelivered order section.
- Respect order revisions, explicit empty-order clears, and round/authority boundaries. Protect the relevant queued orders during queue-pressure eviction without moving them across lifecycle or event barriers.
- Fix the restored Bot roster function being called with the unsupported `required_tiers` keyword. Keep human vehicle tiers included through the existing caller logic and retain the already corrected nonempty-roster startup path.
- Report the underlying roster-preparation exception instead of hiding an interface failure behind a misleading unavailable-roster message.

## Diagnostics, packaging and release scope

- Add bounded catalog-contact and sight-contact evidence to ordinary error reports, including the actual sweep/ray, object bounds and native surface evidence where available. Diagnostics do not decide collision or visibility results.
- Keep offline navigation-baker work and regression fixtures in the source tree, but deliberately ship the original map assets described above. Source-side experimental tools are not evidence that an experimental graph is active in the release.
- Update the launcher, Windows server, client mod, executable version resources and build identity to **0.9.4**.
- Keep the playable ZIP clean: launcher executable, `_internal`, README and license files only. Build proofs, test logs and generated diagnostic files are not prepacked into the user's runtime folder.
- The selected Bot, maps and gameplay are unchanged from `a3e4c593` during version promotion. This expanded changelog documents fixes already present in the published package; it does not replace that package or introduce another gameplay revision.

## Installation

Close the previous launcher and both game processes. Extract the **whole** Windows ZIP into a **new empty folder**, run `wot-0.9.22-offline-battles.exe`, and select the existing #1513 game directory. Do not merge old and new `_internal` folders. No separate Python installation or save reset is required.

## Validation and remaining limits

The release build checks the selected source and original map hashes, targeted historical Bot/mission-integration boundaries, radio and nonempty-roster startup regressions, Python 2.7 bytecode, executable versions, installation and bundled-server readiness. Targeted regression checks are not a claim that the entire current development test suite passes after intentional rollbacks, or that every mission and traffic situation has been verified in the native game. Mutual yielding remains in this original-policy release; remaining jams and frame pacing require gameplay testing.

# v0.9.0 — Personal campaigns, garage services and gameplay fixes

This release includes all changes since **v0.8.4**. It adds personal-campaign progression and reward management, native garage services, training rooms, expanded reserves and daily goals, together with the subsequent gameplay and stability fixes.

Supported game client: **Chinese HD World of Tanks 0.9.22.0.1 #1513**. The launcher runs on 64-bit Windows; the supported game client remains 32-bit.

## Personal campaigns and orders

- Restore personal-mission selection across all five vehicle classes, with selection slots independent of garage capacity. Replacing a mission in the same class works without allowing conflicting simultaneous selections.
- Read mission requirements and rewards from the installed client. Evaluate supported battle conditions and settle main, honors and operation rewards once, including after restarting or receiving a delayed battle result.
- Support completion and reward records for all **300 missions** in the StuG IV, T28 Concept, T 55A and Object 260 operations. The launcher editor provides incomplete, completed and completed-with-honors states, plus group completion, honors and reset actions.
- Automatically complete required earlier missions without granting honors. Clearing a main completion also resets its chain's final mission and all five classes in later dependent operations. Clearing honors alone leaves other missions unchanged.
- Keep edits while switching operations and vehicle classes. Apply saved edits on the next garage load and make reset missions selectable for replay.
- Deliver female crew rewards through the native nationality, vehicle and role selector; unclaimed choices remain available after restarting.
- Show the active mission for the current vehicle in the native battle TAB view. Display campaign progress and rewards in the battle-result quest area and system messages.
- Derive orders from unique honored-final rewards, with a maximum of **20 earned orders** across the four operations. Remove unsupported extras from the old manual order input.
- Record orders committed to individual missions, use the native one-order/four-order skip costs, and return committed orders once when the appropriate honors or reset conditions are met. Repeated completion, reset or refund cannot create extra orders.
- Recalculate campaign badge eligibility from current completion and honors requirements. Removing those requirements also removes an ineligible equipped badge.

## Reversible rewards and launcher notifications

- Resetting a mission withdraws its recorded main and honors rewards, including dependent operation rewards. Resetting honors withdraws only the additional reward. Successful withdrawal allows a later completion to earn the reward again.
- Withdraw reward vehicles that were actually granted, returning their crew and fitted items to storage. Preserve vehicles that were already owned before the reward.
- If the same reward tank is already in the garage, grant its **full original catalogue value in credits**, including the existing gold-to-credit conversion where applicable. Compensation does not use the discounted selling price or a custom bond-shop price. Cancellation withdraws the recorded compensation.
- Limit currency, Free XP, consumable and other quantity withdrawals to the balance or depot stock that remains. Spending the reward no longer blocks cancellation or makes a balance negative. Withdrawal messages report the amount actually removed.
- Preserve mounted items, occupied garage/barracks capacity and orders committed to other missions. Reclaim only remaining mission-earned premium time; elapsed time and separately purchased premium time are preserved.
- Recognize reward women permanently removed after recovery expiry or eviction from the recovery list, so a missing permanently dismissed member no longer prevents a mission reset. Unrelated crew are never substituted.
- Retain the existing dismissed-crew policy: **100 gold for recovery immediately after dismissal, a seven-day recovery window, and at most 100 recovery entries**. The oldest entry is permanently removed when the list overflows.
- Preserve the old progress and property when a reset cannot be resolved safely, such as unknown legacy reward provenance or insufficient space for crew returned from a reward tank. Show the failure in the launcher editor and system messages.
- Add persistent in-game system messages for launcher vehicle, crew, badge and wallet edits, mission grants, withdrawals and duplicate-tank compensation. Messages are committed with saved changes and do not repeat after unchanged edits or accepted delivery.
- Restore the launcher's original Garage vehicles catalogue and simple vehicle/tier labels. Add badge ownership editing using the installed client's names and catalogue, and clear the equipped selection when ownership is removed.

## Armory, vehicle purchases and recovery

- Fix premium vehicles incorrectly requiring research before purchase in the technology tree and regular Shop. Keep hidden reward vehicles separate from ordinary purchase availability.
- Add a native **Special Offers** page dedicated to bond vehicles, using the game's vehicle rows, artwork and purchase confirmation. Offers include a garage slot and fully trained crew; owned vehicles are shown separately and cannot be purchased again.
- Include the eight vehicles from the first official bond-shop assortment that exist in this client, plus five supported retired definitions: **Aufkl. Panther, Waffentrager E 100, SU-122-54, Object 263B and Object 430B**. The retired vehicles also remain available for manual player use in the launcher and are excluded from Bot lineups.
- Keep only vehicle, class, tier, owned and rental controls in Special Offers. Remove irrelevant purchase/recovery/research and non-vehicle controls. Save its filters independently of the regular Shop.
- Correct regular Shop filtering for unresearched, owned and rental vehicles, including combined selections. Prevent stale tab caching from showing the regular Shop inside Special Offers. The regular Shop's offers selector now opens the complete bond catalogue correctly.
- Record eligible premium-vehicle sales in the native recovery list. Recovery costs the hull's selling value plus **10% in credits** and requires a free garage slot. Regular-shop premiums have a **72-hour** recovery window; eligible premiums outside that assortment have no deadline.
- Fix the endless **Updating Store** overlay when switching from vehicle recovery to purchases. Complete missing filter fields, repair incomplete saved filters and release failed requests' loading overlays across the shared Shop, Inventory and Special Offers paths.

## Personal Reserves and daily goals

- Use the native reserve inventory, mission and purchase tabs, with working filters, artwork, tooltips, purchase, activation, Close and Escape actions.
- Offer **44 historical bonus/duration combinations** across Combat XP, Crew XP, Free XP and Credits, including 1-, 2-, 4- and 6-hour variants. Existing reserve balances and activation records are retained.
- Expose all **three active slots** in both reserve panels. Run up to three different resource types together; a stronger reserve can replace an active reserve of the same type after confirmation.
- Persist purchases, stock and timers. Timers continue while offline, and battle bonuses use eligibility at battle start, including results arriving after a reserve expires or is replaced.
- Select three daily offline goals from battle-count, damage and victory templates at **00:00 UTC / 08:00 Beijing time**. Each template has a fixed reserve reward; reopening or restarting does not reroll the day or grant rewards twice.
- Count a destroyed player's contribution when the battle eventually settles, even if the player has returned to the garage. A voluntary live abandonment does not advance daily goals or consume the daily first-win bonus.
- Show completed daily goals with the native green completion tick and reward block, a combined completion popup and system messages. Reward labels consistently say **Combat XP reserve, Crew XP reserve, Free XP reserve or Credits reserve**, matching the daily list.

## Premium account, first wins and battle income

- Restore premium purchase handling, prices, expiry extension and persistence using the client's original **360-, 180-, 30-, 7-, 3- and 1-day** options and artwork.
- Apply the legacy premium-account **50% credit and XP bonus** to actual battle income, including crew training, and show the corresponding standard/premium result columns. Eligibility is fixed at battle start.
- Add a per-vehicle **x2 XP bonus for the first standard-battle victory each day**. Garage cards and native tooltips show the available bonus, remove it after that tank wins, and restore it at the daily reset. Losses and draws do not consume it.
- Keep reserve and save-configured income bonuses separate from original battle earnings. Use consistent rounding and values in both deposited rewards and native result calculations.
- Preserve the client's friendly-fire result labels: credit penalties and compensation, and XP penalties. Previously saved battles are not repriced or paid again.

## Battle commendations and results

- Award and persist all eleven supported small battle-result commendations: **Shellproof, Fire for Effect, Fighter, Duelist, Demolition Expert, Arsonist, Bruiser, Hand of God, Eye for an Eye, Spotter and Battle Buddy**.
- Carry these awards through personal result ribbons, public results and career records. Include the required module-only damage, fire damage, ammunition-rack destruction and mutual-kill facts.
- Preserve Battle Buddy's account-wide 50-battle clean series and Spotter's achievement/best-result records across vehicles, restarts and repeated result delivery.
- Distinguish surviving, watching the battle to its end, returning after destruction and abandoning a live vehicle, so result presentation and daily eligibility use the appropriate facts.

## Commander voices, crew skills and combat state

- Preserve the mounted commander's gender when entering battle. Female commanders use female voice selection in both localized Standard mode and national Commander mode, while retaining native language and special-voice selection.
- Apply Standard/Commander changes immediately during battle, including after sound previews and subsequent settings changes.
- Stop repeatedly initializing unavailable online voice-chat services when opening offline sound settings.
- Fix fourth-slot directives being omitted from the visible gun's reload calculation. The battle now uses the accepted mounted loadout, including equipment, crew skills, food and directives.
- Preserve reload completion percentage when **Intuition** successfully switches ammunition during an active reload; it no longer instantly finishes an unfinished reload. A loaded round still switches immediately on a successful activation.
- Refresh reload timing and targeting parameters when stun starts or clears. Prevent repaired tracks from being overwritten by stale damage state and remaining falsely destroyed.
- Prevent the native target monitor from prematurely clearing the offline **Expert** module overlay while valid focus continues.
- Keep the death camera on the player's wreck when the killer was not visible in the death snapshot, preserving hidden-enemy information.
- Drive pivot-turn engine RPM from track movement so turning in place has the appropriate engine response.

## Bots, maps and destructible collisions

- Improve recovery from repeated terrain blockage by treating a continuously blocked corridor as one event instead of restarting recovery while the hull wags.
- Choose a geometrically valid forward or reverse escape from close vehicle contact, using consistent short-distance terrain and vehicle checks to reduce sustained overlap and failed escape attempts.
- Reroute the western **Serene Coast** path around the parked train and update the baked navigation data.
- Recognize equivalent duplicate destructible placements on **D-Day** using their live identities. Use the mounted chassis/hull bounds, pitch and roll in contact checks instead of an unmounted hull box.
- Strengthen partially destroyed building collision handling for translation and turning. Retain solid residual structures, prevent entry during model replacement and permit bounded outward escape from an existing overlap.
- Improve **Ruinberg** destructible identification and scanning under load. An isolated invalid item no longer disables unrelated, independently verified cars, motorcycles, benches or fences in the same chunk; chunk reloads retire stale identification state.
- Correct collision filtering after destruction for reported **Ruinberg flowerbeds and Westfield fences**. Remove accepted original collision surfaces while retaining real damaged-model collision and unrelated intact obstacles.

## Siege mode, stability and diagnostics

- Stabilize copied Siege-mode body/ground offsets and keep the rendered body, aiming and shot direction on the same pose provider.
- Restore the initial Siege-mode key hint and retain it after the native tutorial counter would otherwise hide it. Display each vehicle's actual next-switch duration in stable modes, while keeping the live countdown during a transition.
- Normalize finite remote rotations before native matrix writes and isolate an individual presentation failure. A failed vehicle pose update can be retried without disconnecting the simulation worker or ending the entire round.
- Fix startup ownership validation when publishing unowned bond offers and correct sound-settings adapter initialization.
- Extend bounded diagnostics for crew publication, kills, crew skills, reload loadouts, destructible contacts, wreck support and Siege transitions. Clear stale suspension diagnostics when changing suspension modes.
- Expand regression coverage for persistence, failed-save rollback, repeated receipts, UI lifecycle, filter transitions and reward accounting. Align the launcher, server, client mod, executable metadata and diagnostic version with **0.9.0**.

## Scope and installation

- Training mode now opens a LAN room with host-selected map, teams, duration and optional Bots. It awards no credits, XP, bonds, medals, mission progress or lifetime statistics. Repairs are free; spent ammunition and consumables are charged normally. Standard maps remain the supported battle maps.
- Bond shopping is an offline extension: the permanent official bond shop postdates 0.9.22. Retired-vehicle prices, reserve prices, daily goals and reward-reset/duplicate-compensation behavior are explicitly defined offline rules, not a claim that the historical Chinese server offered identical services.
- All 300 campaign completion states and reward definitions are supported by the editor; automatic battle completion still requires sufficient authoritative facts. Conditions needing unavailable timing, visibility, stun history or platoon information remain unevaluated.
- Older saves without vehicle-sale records or reward provenance cannot reconstruct those missing historical records. New commendations do not reconstruct missing facts from old battles.
- Native Windows rendering, audible voice-bank selection and physical behavior still require gameplay acceptance on the supported client. Strv S1 travel stutter and exact crushed-car support contours are not claimed resolved by this release.
- Existing saves and settings can be retained. Close the game, server and old launcher, extract **wot-0.9.22-offline-battles-0.9.0-Windows-x64.zip** completely into a new folder, then use the new launcher to install the matching mod. Keep the extracted files together.

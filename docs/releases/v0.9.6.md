# Version 0.9.6

This release brings together the gameplay fixes tested after v0.9.5. The main work is vehicle physics: collisions between players and Bots, pushing and rotating wrecks, contact with bridges and cliffs, and braking. It also includes ammunition and sound fixes, lower ammo-rack explosion overhead, garage repairs, Bot editor improvements and more complete battle notifications.

Supported game client: **Chinese HD World of Tanks 0.9.22.0.1 #1513, 32-bit**. The Windows launcher and bundled server are x64. Other client builds are not supported.

## Player and Bot collisions

- Correct asymmetric collision responses in which a light Bot could repeatedly displace a much heavier player. Both sides use mounted vehicle mass, engine-derived motion and the shared contact result.
- Correct delayed or duplicated shove impulses across worker updates. A crowded series of contacts can no longer spend the same incoming momentum repeatedly.
- Improve opposing powered contact, including driving or reversing into a moving Bot. Bot route recovery and track holding no longer silently override an accepted physical shove.
- Keep live players as physical obstacles while consuming their contact reports. Pending movement is not treated as space already cleared by the other vehicle.
- Sweep the hull through its travel against other tanks, wrecks and solid world geometry. This reduces tunnelling, pushing a wreck through another Bot, and displacing a Bot into a wall or rock.
- Improve oblique contact, tangential movement and escape from existing overlap, instead of reducing every push to forward/backward/sideways movement.
- Repair intermittent missing ram damage at solid hull and chassis corners. Use the armour plate established at the first impact and the horizontal closing speed; later overlap correction must not invent a second impact.
- Keep the current offline damage calibration: friendly ramming causes no HP loss and enemy ram damage uses the existing 25% scale. These are project settings, not a claim that the private retail damage formula has been recovered.

## Wreck movement and bridge support

- Repeated pushes continue to reach wrecks after the first contact. Wrecks retain their original vehicle mass rather than acquiring arbitrary resistance after death.
- Couple linear movement and rotation so off-centre pushes can turn a wreck. Check the swept hull against static geometry while turning.
- Continue vertical integration after a wreck stops moving horizontally, allowing unsupported wrecks to fall.
- Separate autonomous route safety from externally forced movement. Bots still avoid dangerous routes when driving themselves; an accepted shove is evaluated as physical movement rather than automatically rejected as a navigation hazard.
- Correct stale bridge-height retention and suspension sleep with inadequate support. A bridge deck detected above a tipped hull must not repeatedly cancel its fall.
- Align Bot and wreck support checks with the player path, including genuine body contacts during a tumble. Do not mistake a rigid-body penetration correction for driving up an impossible step.
- Preserve downhill and lateral movement when horizontal world contact occurs, instead of clearing the entire falling motion.
- Support the full rotation range in state transmission. Use continuous angle interpolation across rotation seams to reduce frozen tilted vehicles and sudden jumps below bridges.
- Withdraw the experimental centre-of-mass transform that caused repeated flipping at a bridge edge. The final release retains the later, tested support path.

The final tests substantially improved pushing and bridge departures. Some brief side contact and specific map positions still need investigation; this release does not promise that every bridge, cliff or air wall is fixed. Retained corpses owned by other human clients still have the existing immovable-owner limitation.

## Driving and braking

- Limit grounded downhill overspeed to **110% of the configured maximum speed**.
- Forward movement followed by S or F, and reverse movement followed by W or R, now applies service braking until the vehicle stops before changing direction. It is no longer merely opposing engine acceleration.
- Set released-drive automatic braking to the accepted **50% service-braking target**, while retaining rolling resistance and slope handling. Handbraking remains separate.
- Preserve the braking feel accepted in the final test builds; removal of diagnostic logging does not retune it.

## Ammunition, aiming and visibility

- Start physical Bot shots, including artillery shots, from the descriptor's gun pivot/breech path. A barrel protruding through a wall must not let a shell begin beyond that wall.
- Preserve the fitted shell and physical launch state for the shot. The garage's selected shell order remains in effect.
- Repair direct HE hit explosion effects and impact sounds, including artillery HE. Damaging direct hits use explosion feedback; a direct non-penetrating hit with no HP damage uses the no-penetration impact outcome. This follow-up changes impact feedback, not crew voice lines.
- Restore one-click firing of descriptor-defined multi-round bursts on early automatic guns. Ammunition is charged when each physical shot is committed; this does not turn every conventional autoloader into an automatic burst weapon.
- Require a valid hit on the target's currently exposed geometry for aiming focus and outline selection. Walls and wrecks occlude the ray; aiming at a visible portion can reveal the whole target outline.
- Refresh target geometry and distance selection from the current vehicle pose rather than stale positions.
- Improve spotting checkpoint placement from the actual hull and turret descriptors, including observer geometry on vehicles with rear-mounted turrets. This is not an increase to every vehicle's view range.

## Ammo-rack explosion performance

- Keep the ammo-rack explosion and detached-turret flight presentation, but make the detached turret **visual-only** to remove costly ongoing collision work.
- Remove detached-turret obstacles from shell, reticle and navigation checks. A landed detached turret cannot be pushed and does not provide cover in this version.
- Reduce redundant turret processing, reuse valid contact evidence and improve internal-layout cache readiness.
- Avoid repeated navigation searches that cannot improve the result, reuse exact-segment results within a callback and avoid clearing an entire cache when one entry is evicted.

Playtests reported a substantial reduction in ammo-rack explosion stalls after turret collision was disabled. This does not eliminate every frame-time or high-ping symptom: hidden-worker suspension and ground queries remain a performance limit in some scenes.

## Bot deployment, routes and editor

- Place SPGs in validated rear spawn slots so artillery turning toward its firing position is less likely to block the vehicles heading to the front.
- Exclude retired vehicles such as SU-122-54 from automatic Bot selection while keeping eligible retired vehicles available to the player.
- Improve narrow-corridor and doorway route clearance using the actual vehicle footprint and native world checks. Improve capture destination selection to use the actual base circle rather than an unrelated waypoint.
- Keep capture movement and local route recovery consistent when control changes. These fixes address shared navigation behaviour; no blanket claim is made that every reported Prague, Ensk or other map obstruction is resolved.
- Filter the Bot editor map by the selected vehicle class. Switching from heavy to medium or light routes hides the other classes without deleting their saved routes.
- Allow waypoint conditions, including a timed wait or a permanent hold, to be edited at the selected point. Saved route conditions are applied by the runtime and can be revised later.

## Garage, crew and supplies

- Restore Armory Special Offers vehicle listings after returning from battle.
- Repair fitting and removing directives in fully unlocked saves. Newly initialized unlocked saves receive the intended starting bonds; existing saves are retained.
- Prefer crew members who can still gain useful training when accelerated crew training is enabled, using a deterministic priority instead of repeatedly rewarding already-complete crew.
- Repair the Removed Speed Governor's on/off switch and active-state echo so other consumables do not incorrectly cancel it.
- Retain the descriptor's 10% engine-power increase and 1.5 engine HP per eligible second, with the tested below-half-base-forward-speed condition and whole-second damage pulses. Engine module HP is not horsepower; a healthy stock KV-5 needs about 120 eligible seconds to reach its yellow threshold.
- Preserve the requested offline settlement rule: an active governor at death is charged; an inactive governor at death or a survived battle is not. The active-at-death loss rule is an offline project choice, not an independently verified retail rule.
- Preserve idempotent settlement and depot-first automatic resupply. Remove temporary governor switch and five-second wear diagnostics after the user confirmed engine damage, including on MT-25.

## Battle notifications and LAN squad presentation

- Complete missing environmental death notices for players, squad members and Bots, including collision deaths, drowning, overturning and death-zone deaths.
- Keep the local player's death in the central postmortem panel, without an extra right-hand kill-feed line. Restore the client's own cause-specific wording for unassisted environmental deaths.
- Keep the local player's enemy kills and ram kills in the native yellow style.
- Use gold name and vehicle fields for other LAN squad players inside the normal green/red event text. Ordinary Bot names retain their native team colours, including friendly-hit warnings.
- Give same-team LAN humans consistent squad presentation in the roster, overhead markers and minimap. Preserve the missing-feed supplement when observing a teammate.
- Avoid replaying kill credit, paired messages or voice feedback while restoring a missing notification.

## Exiting the game and save preservation

- Repair a tutorial cleanup error caused by an expired interface reference. The original tutorial cleanup can now finish and allow the later replay, voice, sound and settings cleanup to run. Other shutdown errors are not silently suppressed.
- Remove a fixed ten-second wait after the visible game and all its child processes have exited. A live replacement game process still keeps the session open. Native Windows tests cover both normal exit and process handoff; whole-game exit time remains dependent on actual client cleanup.
- Isolate launcher maintenance tests from real user save directories. Verify that normal updates, forced reinstalls and startup repair preserve both default and named save slots. Clearing offline progress remains a separate, explicitly confirmed action.

## Internal modules and remaining limitations

Download **WoT-0.9.6-Unresolved-Internal-Modules-English.docx** separately from the release assets. It lists every unresolved entry in the current audit: **22 vehicles with unavailable internal layouts**, **8 with authored reconstructions that still need verification**, and **176 decoded entries with partial module or crew coverage gaps**.

The partial list mainly concerns observation devices and turret rotators. Some fixed-gun vehicles do not require a rotating-turret component. It must not be read as 176 missing ammo racks. External armour, tracks and guns use separate collision sources. Increasing module damage cannot create absent internal hit geometry or bypass penetration and module saving throws.

No guessed layouts were added in these follow-ups. Missing-layout remediation, canonical module/crew injuries from falls, fully playable offline replay recording and remaining map air walls are not claimed complete. The LT-14 investigation did not justify a mission-rule change. Some legacy Bot aiming/navigation regression tests remain failing; the general CI workflow is not fully green.

## Installation

Close the old game and launcher. Extract **wot-0.9.22-offline-battles-0.9.6-Windows-x64.zip** into a new empty folder, then run its launcher and select the supported game client. Do not mix old and new `_internal` directories or manually add another mod copy. All LAN participants must use the same package. Existing save slots do not need to be reset.

The release package is built from the selected main-branch commit on GitHub's x64 Windows runner. Version, client payload, simulated installation and bundled-service checks verify the download. Automated checks and the completed playtests do not certify every vehicle, map or native physics interaction.

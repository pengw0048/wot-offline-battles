# v0.8.3 — Bonds, directives, crew skills and battle feedback

This release brings the September 15 gameplay fixes to the Chinese HD client
**World of Tanks 0.9.22.0.1 #1513**. The launcher and MOD version is **0.8.3**;
the supported game client remains unchanged.

## Artillery hit voices

- Direct hits that cause HP damage now use the penetration voice, even when
  the HE shell did not physically penetrate the armour.
- Splash damage without a direct hit uses the splash-hit voice.
- Direct hits that cause no HP damage use the non-penetration voice.
- Voice selection does not change physical penetration or damage statistics.

## Expert and crew skills

- Restore Expert feedback for damaged modules and injured crew after aiming
  at a visible, living enemy for four seconds. Clear stale indicators when
  changing targets, losing visibility or losing the active skill.
- Connect Smooth Ride, Snap Shot and Safe Stowage to their battle effects,
  including the corresponding directives.
- Add native conditional effects for Adrenaline Rush, Preventative Maintenance
  and Armorer. Effects follow the relevant crew member's injury and recovery
  state; the worker's accepted reload timing matches the visible client.

## Automatic vehicle selection

- Both teams use a shared vehicle-tier and class template, but draw their
  actual models independently. Opposing models may match or differ.
- Keep unusable-model exclusions and the host's excluded-vehicle list.
- Explicit custom lineups remain supported.

## Directives and bonds

- Enable all 15 directives in the fourth consumable slot, with bond purchases,
  mounting and a separate automatic-resupply setting.
- Consume one mounted directive per battle. Repeated settlement of the same
  battle does not consume it or award its rewards again.
- Price improved equipment in bonds: toolbox 3,000; optics 4,000; rammer,
  ventilation, stabilizer and gun-laying drive 5,000 each.
- Directives cost 2–12 bonds each. Paid removal of improved equipment costs
  200 bonds; selling improved equipment returns credits, not bonds.
- Award eligible medal bonds using the tier-dependent schedule introduced in
  9.20.1 and retained in 0.9.22. Record rewards and directive service costs in
  the battle results and persistent balance. Premium status and offline
  credit/XP multipliers do not multiply bond rewards.
- Add bond-balance editing to the launcher's custom save editor. Older saves
  without a bond balance start at zero.

## Not included in this release

- The additional base-XP bond payout for all-Tier-X battles is not implemented:
  a reliable historical conversion table is still missing. Medal rewards are
  available; ranked-season and campaign rewards are outside the offline mode.
- Grand Battles (30 versus 30) remain disabled. The required large-map
  navigation/spawn data and complete 60-vehicle battle, UI and result support
  are not yet available.
- A complete radio-contact-distance model is not implemented. Relaying has
  no effective consumer; Signal Boosting changes its parameter but does not
  currently limit the team's shared spotting by radio distance.

## Installation and validation

- Exit the old launcher, server and game. Extract
  `wot-0.9.22-offline-battles-0.8.3-Windows-x64.zip` into a new folder, then run
  `wot-0.9.22-offline-battles.exe` directly from that folder.
- Select the existing supported game directory and reinstall the client MOD
  through the launcher. Existing external saves and user settings are retained.
- The download needs only one extraction. These notes are included as
  `UPDATE_NOTES.md`.
- Launcher branding, executable file/product versions, server and MOD versions,
  package metadata and diagnostic identities are aligned to 0.8.3.
- The release workflow runs client and launcher regression tests, builds the
  Python 2.7 payload and Windows x64 executables, and verifies versions,
  embedded payloads and the final ZIP before publication. These checks do not
  replace in-game acceptance of voices, native UI and battle behaviour on #1513.

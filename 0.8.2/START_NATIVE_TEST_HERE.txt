WORLD OF TANKS 0.8.2 NATIVE BOT PHYSICS EXPERIMENT 1.8.59
==========================================================

This is a separate experimental build. It keeps the retail 0.8.2 rigid-body
physics for authority-side bots; replicas still consume network snapshots.

Install
-------

1. Close the game.
2. Keep <game root>\offhangar_user.
3. Delete or move aside <game root>\res_mods\0.8.2.
4. Copy this package's complete 0.8.2 folder into res_mods.

Do not merge individual files. The package must not leave an old
mod_offhangar.pyc behind.

What changed in 1.8.59
----------------------

- Bots that yield to a stationary player or crossing route now keep a bounded
  traffic-wait timer. A prolonged standstill makes only the yielding bot use
  the existing staggered reverse-and-turn recovery instead of waiting forever;
  vehicle collision and right-of-way remain enabled.
- Closing LAN SETTINGS now restores the stock garage cursor provider.
- The packaged Windows LAN server can request a UAC-approved inbound rule for
  its own executable on TCP 28782 from any remote address.

What changed in 1.8.57
----------------------

- Player-to-vehicle collision volume is active again. A tick-local module
  shadowing error left both the Python-player/native-bot contact resolver and
  the lower-hull terrain classifier unbound at the time they ran. That allowed
  friendly/enemy overlap, while the terrain classifier failed closed and made
  an ordinary lower-hull slope contact feel like a wall. Failures are now
  counted and reported once per battle instead of being silently swallowed.
- Cross-slope terrain classification now profiles the exact lower-hull lane
  that touched the ground instead of substituting the chassis centre line.
  Continuous track lanes remain drivable while an abrupt step seen by either
  track remains solid. Engine power and the retail climb limit are unchanged.

What changed in 1.8.56
----------------------

- Terrain-only contacts from the player's lower hull sweep no longer consume
  the bounded solid-contact budget or turn a flat, shallow ridge into a wall.
  The same lane still checks every non-terrain object and rejects a genuinely
  over-limit longitudinal terrain face.
- Native activation monitoring now skips the player proxy and malformed
  non-bot entries instead of resetting a valid 29-bot count to zero.

What changed in 1.8.55
----------------------

- Player collision sampling now recognizes gradual descents and rounded bumps
  as terrain while continuing the same exact hull sweep beyond the first slope
  hit, so a thin wall or fence behind that hit still blocks. Rejected upper
  surfaces are followed downward only to a genuinely upward-facing floor.
- A simulation authority now holds native-bot placement until every current
  bot pose has real streamed collision support. The coverage range is derived
  from the full playable bounds, retained while native owners exist, and also
  gates a LAN authority handoff; baked height is never substituted for a live
  collision result.
- The wider coverage intentionally keeps the playable map's collision chunks
  resident and can raise the render far plane to roughly 600-1000 m. Windows
  FPS and 32-bit working-set usage are therefore explicit acceptance checks.

What changed in 1.8.54
----------------------

- Player slope sampling now continues below low fences, poles and trees instead
  of treating their top surfaces as a 35-40 degree terrain grade. Forward or
  reverse probing at rest carries no invented collision speed.
- Ferdinand presentation diagnostics now distinguish a canonical rigid-body
  pulse, chassis-provider split, HP_gui-node split and fashion placement pulse.
  The diagnostic is threshold-triggered and capped; it does not smooth or move
  the vehicle.
- Friendly-traffic waits are now reported separately from server-order waits,
  making brief deployment congestion visible without changing right-of-way or
  native collision behavior.

What changed in 1.8.53
----------------------

- Canonical spawn selection now proves terrain support under the complete
  vehicle footprint. The Airfield artillery slot that straddled a steep edge
  is moved to a flat, fully supported pose before the native body is created.
- Structures such as houses and base ruins are no longer erased from the
  navigation bake. Runtime corridor probes only pass a structure when the
  current vehicle satisfies the retail mass, speed, scale and health crush
  test.
- Attack lanes stop at a full-hull-clear point inside the capture circle. A
  blocked final approach may use one bounded graph detour, while near-goal
  server orders can no longer bypass blocked or hazardous links.

What changed in 1.8.52
----------------------

- Player ramming now resolves every static contact with the retail hit-point and
  surface-normal material probe. Long fences are no longer approximated by one
  endpoint, and failed or unidentified destruction remains solid.
- Destructibles now use the retail mass, speed, scale and health crush test, and
  commit local authoritative state only after one native delivery succeeds.
- Dense foliage maps use mathematically exact visibility bounds and stop volume
  traversal at the historical 0.60 camouflage cap. Spotting rules and cadence
  are unchanged; only redundant geometry work is skipped.

What changed in 1.8.51
----------------------

- All 33 stock maps now ship canonical 15-versus-15 spawn poses baked against
  the largest supported vehicle footprint and static or destructible scenery.
  The Port truck and car overlaps that froze two bots are no longer spawnable.
- LAN clients wait for a server-accepted, immutable lineup before creating any
  bot. Every client consumes the same pose instead of re-projecting from a roof.
- Authority bot rendering now follows the same native physics-root pose used by
  gameplay and networking, removing the delayed provider catch-up seen at range.
- Player ground support skips the short-lived collision skin of a destructible
  that was just crushed, so its old top surface can no longer lift the tank.

Test
----

Start one normal offline or LAN battle. No debug key is required. If bots still
do not move, exit normally and send python.log plus the server log. The useful
rows begin with:

    NATIVE_BOT_PHYSICS ground_wait
    NATIVE_BOT_PHYSICS hazard_recovery
    NATIVE_BOT_PHYSICS drive_diagnostic
    NATIVE_BOT_PRESENTATION jump
    LAN NET
    SERVER PERF

In particular, check both teams' spawn areas. Startup should reach
`prepared=29 active=29 failed=0`, with no bot repeating `ground_wait` at the
distant base. Also repeat the reported Lakeville road and Karelia ridge tests:
gradual up/down slopes and rounded bumps should move normally, while a real
wall still blocks. Watch FPS and process working set after the full lineup is
active because the authority deliberately retains whole-playable-map collision
coverage until all native owners are released.

Also check the aiming fix in arcade view: the visible sight and the point under
the mouse should agree at minimum zoom. Hold the mouse still over a distant
bush or vehicle and move only the wheel; the world point should remain fixed.
At long range, the small marker should sit on the first ballistic impact rather
than on the elevated barrel line; entering sniper view and firing should agree
with that point. Briefly aim into empty sky as well and confirm that FPS does not
drop materially. The PERF line now separates `player_gun_marker` and reports
`marker_vehicle_candidates`; the real Windows client remains the final
performance gate for the bounded Python trajectory preview.

The drive diagnostic waits about one second after input and reports both the
C++ physics root and Entity presentation pose plus their deltas. The native
bridge automatically rejects an unsupported WorldOfTanks.exe. This
experiment still requires Windows/BigWorld runtime acceptance before it can
replace the stable Python-physics build.
